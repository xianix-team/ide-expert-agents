# Market Triggers: Norway, EU, and EEA

Used in Identify to spot documents and product features a market may call for, and in Review to phrase questions. **Leads for the customer's lawyers, not statements of law.** Last reviewed **November 2026**. Items marked † rest on secondary sources. Never quote a row as a legal requirement: ask the lawyers to confirm it.

## Which situation?

Norway is in the **EEA, not the EU**. An EU act applies in Norway only once it is in the EEA Agreement and implemented in Norwegian law. Selling into an EU country can bring the EU act in earlier.

| Situation | Use |
|---|---|
| Norwegian publisher, users only in Norway | "Norway" rows. Acts not yet Norwegian law: list as *upcoming* |
| Users or customers in EU countries | "Norway" rows plus the "EU users" column, plus national rules in those countries for the lawyers |
| Anywhere outside the EEA (including the UK and Switzerland) | Not covered. Ask which lawyers cover that market |

## Norway

| If the product… | It points to | Ask the lawyers about |
|---|---|---|
| Processes personal data | Privacy policy, DPA where one party processes for another | GDPR through *personopplysningsloven*. National identity numbers and children's consent have specific rules |
| Uses cookies, SDKs, or device storage beyond the strictly necessary | Cookie information, and **consent before they run** | *Ekomloven* § 3-15, GDPR-standard consent since 1 January 2025 (Nkom and Datatilsynet supervise) |
| Sells online to consumers | Terms, withdrawal and cancellation information, clear provider details | *Angrerettloven*, *digitalytelsesloven*, *avtaleloven* §§ 36–37, *markedsføringsloven*, *ehandelsloven* |
| Sends marketing messages | Consent and opt-out handling | *Markedsføringsloven* § 15 |
| Is a **public-sector** site or app | Accessibility statement, made in uustatus.no and updated yearly | Uutilsynet's rules, and language duties (bokmål/nynorsk) |
| Is bought by a **public body** | SSA contract annexes (e.g. SSA-L, SSA-V, SSA-D, cloud) instead of a separate SLA or support agreement | Which SSA and version, plus *offentleglova* and *arkivlova* on confidentiality and retention |
| Keeps invoices or payments | Retention exceptions to any deletion promise | *Bokføringsloven* |
| Runs in a critical sector, health, or finance | Security, incident, outsourcing, and sector terms | *Digitalsikkerhetsloven*, *Normen*, DORA, with specialist lawyers |
| Is **accessed from outside the EEA** by development, support, or operations | Transfer statements in the privacy policy, DPA, sub-processor list, and support agreement | Datatilsynet treats access by a separate company outside the EEA as a transfer. "Stored in Norway" doesn't answer it |

## EU acts with a different status in Norway

| Act | EU users: may point to | Norway, at last review |
|---|---|---|
| AI Act, Art 50 | AI notice in the product | *KI-loven* not adopted, bill reported for spring 2027† |
| Withdrawal function ("angreknapp") | In-product withdrawal function, applies in the EU from 19 June 2026† | Adopted 2026, entry into force delayed† |
| Data Act | Switching, export, and exit terms for hosted services | Not in Norwegian law in 2026† |
| Accessibility Act | Accessibility information for consumer services | Not in Norwegian law. Norwegian sellers into the EU must follow it |
| Cyber Resilience Act | Vulnerability disclosure, support period, update terms. EU reporting since 11 September 2026 | Not in the EEA Agreement on 11 September 2026 |
| Digital Services Act | Content rules, reporting, complaints for user content | *Digitaltjenesteloven* delayed† |
| Digital Omnibus (GDPR, cookies) | Nothing yet, still under negotiation† | Nothing yet |
| ODR platform | Closed 20 July 2025. A remaining link is a Review finding | Same |

Official sources for the engineer or lawyers to check (this skill doesn't browse): lovdata.no, europalov.no, eur-lex.europa.eu, datatilsynet.no, nkom.no, forbrukertilsynet.no, uutilsynet.no, anskaffelser.no.

## Stores and platforms

| Platform | Points to |
|---|---|
| Apple App Store | Privacy policy link in the listing and the app, in-app account deletion, privacy labels, Apple's standard EULA or a custom one meeting its minimum terms |
| Google Play | Privacy policy at a public, non-geofenced, non-PDF URL, Data safety form, account deletion in the app and by web link |
| Other marketplaces | The publisher documentation's required URLs and terms. Ask the engineer for it |
| AI providers | Usage policy or disclosure that must be passed on to users |
